Mithai Mile

Eleven cities. Eleven sweets. One song each, playing on the counter as you walk past.

scroll

Kolkata, West Bengal

Howrah Bridge

Rasgulla & Rasmalai

Spongy chhena balls dunked in sugar syrup or slow-thickened milk, the pride of every mishti shop on the block.

Ekla Cholo Re — Rabindranath Tagore, sung by Anupam Roy

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Hawa Mahal

Ghevar

A disc of fried batter honeycombed with air pockets, soaked in syrup and made only for Teej, exactly when this song is sung.

Kesariya Balam Aavo Ni — Rajasthani folk, sung by Sarita Kharwal

Delhi

Red Fort

Ladoo

Besan, boondi, or motichoor, hand-rolled outside every Chandni Chowk mithai shop. The default sweet for good news.

Dilli Wali Girlfriend — Yeh Jawaani Hai Deewani, Arijit Singh & Sunidhi Chauhan

Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal

Petha

Translucent ash-gourd cubes candied in sugar syrup, sold outside the Taj since the Mughal era.

Pyar Kiya Toh Darna Kya — Mughal-E-Azam, Lata Mangeshkar

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Dashashwamedh Ghat

Malaiyo

Whipped, dew-set milk foam sold only on winter mornings on the ghats. Dissolves before it fully lands on the tongue.

Banarasiya — Raanjhanaa, A.R. Rahman & Shreya Ghoshal

Amritsar, Punjab

Golden Temple

Pinni

Whole wheat, ghee, and nuts rolled into a dense winter ladoo, packed into every tiffin a Punjabi son takes leaving home.

Laung Laachi — title track, sung by Mannat Noor

Mysore, Karnataka

Mysore Palace

Mysore Pak

Ghee, gram flour, and sugar, invented in the Mysore Palace kitchen in the 1930s and never bettered since.

Belageddu — Kirik Party, sung by Vijay Prakash

Hyderabad, Telangana

Charminar

Double Ka Meetha

Fried bread soaked in saffron milk and topped with dry fruits, the Nizami answer to bread pudding.

Naatu Naatu — RRR, Rahul Sipligunj & Kaala Bhairava

Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu

Nellaiappar Temple

Iruttukadai Halwa

Wheat halwa first sold in the dark ("iruttu") because the shop had no electricity. The name outlived the blackout by a century.

Rowdy Baby — Maari 2, Dhanush & Dhee

Patna, Bihar

Golghar

Thekua

Wheat and jaggery, deep-fried and stamped with a wooden mould. The sweet that travels in the Chhath prasad basket.

Kanch Hi Baans Ke Bahangiya — Chhath geet, sung by Kalpana Patowary

Pune, Maharashtra

Shaniwar Wada

Modak

Steamed rice-flour dumplings stuffed with jaggery and coconut. Ganpati Bappa's favourite, made fresh every Chaturthi.

Sukhkarta Dukhharta — Ganpati Aarti, sung by Lata Mangeshkar
